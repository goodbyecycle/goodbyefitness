Months on record

    How this pays out

    • Bonus is paid on the gain over the previous month — this month's figure minus last month's.
    • A month that goes backwards pays $0, never a negative bonus.
    • Google reviews counts positive reviews only — four stars and up — and reads the date on each review, so past months come out exact.
    • Previous-month figures carry forward automatically once a month is saved.
    • Rows tagged auto were filled in by a sync, not by hand. A sync never overwrites anything typed in.
    • A row set to pay on the month's own total ignores the gain and pays the rate on this month's figure.
    • Instagram and Facebook follower counts are snapshotted nightly — a month closes on its last snapshot, so history starts the day you connect.
    • Website visitors are counted by the site itself — unique per day, bots excluded, no tracking cookie. The row pays nothing until a rate is set.
    • Only an admin login can change rates or mark a month paid.
