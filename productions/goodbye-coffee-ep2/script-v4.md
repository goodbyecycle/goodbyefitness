# Goodbye Coffee — Ep. 2 · Script v4 (a young woman on the couch)

**Rev 2 changes:**
- Shot 10, the flashback, is cut. Its line now plays over 09.
- After Sterling says the quote, the quote card appears, then the picture fades back in on the young woman looking at her phone. The mug says "Probably not.", then the end card.

**Voices:** MUG = narrator · STERLING = Sterling's own voice · WOMAN = a new young female voice · BARISTA = Maya. New stills are marked **NEW**.

```
[01 — Door opens, snow]
MUG: The bell over the door rang, and the cold came in with him.
Sterling.
It's been a month since that day at the doctor's office.
A month doesn't sound like much.
It is when you're running out of them.

[02 — In line, on his cane]
MUG: Overcoat buttoned to the collar. Newsboy cap. A cane now.
He didn't have the cane last month.
His coat hung loose. His face had grown thin.

[03 — Barista pours]
MUG: The girl behind the counter filled me to the top and smiled.

[04 — Mug slid across the counter]
BARISTA: "Careful. It's hot."

[05 — His hands around the mug]
MUG: He wrapped both hands around me.
His fingers were cold. And they shook.

[06 — Walk to the couch]
MUG: Tap of the cane. A pause. Another tap.

[07 — Sits, sets the mug down]
MUG: He lowered himself into the couch and opened his journal.

[NEW A — Young woman with her coffee at the other end of the couch]
MUG: The place was packed that morning.
WOMAN: "Is anyone sitting here?"
STERLING: "Just me and my coffee. Go ahead."

[08 — Journal: "Devin"]
MUG: At the top of the page, he wrote one word.
Devin.

[NEW B — Close on her, glancing at the page]
WOMAN: "Who's Devin?"

[NEW C — Two-shot, Sterling talking, pen on the page]
STERLING: "My little brother. Haven't spoken to him in twelve years."

[09 — Sterling at the snowy window]
STERLING: "Our father's funeral. Something got said. Wrong tone, wrong day.
Could've settled it right there in the parking lot.
But I got in my car. And so did he."

[11 — Montage: chair, phone, invitation]
STERLING: "Then I missed a Christmas. Then two.
He got married, and I heard about it from a cousin."
MUG: Twelve years.

[NEW D — Close on her, both hands around her cup, moved]
WOMAN: "What was the fight about?"

[12 — Close-up, wet eyes, at the window]
STERLING: "...I don't even remember."
MUG: Twelve years without his brother, and he couldn't even remember why.

[13 — Cold mug]
MUG: His coffee went cold. He never noticed.

[14 — Journal page]
MUG: He wrote beneath his brother's name,
pressing so hard the pen nearly tore the paper.

[NEW E — Two-shot, Sterling turns to her, eye to eye]
STERLING: "Let me tell you something, young lady."

(pause)

[QUOTE CARD — fades in; Sterling reads it over the card]
STERLING (V.O.): "Resolve the incident when it happens.
Anger and resentment only collect interest.
Eventually the loan will come due and you'll forget what the loan was for."

[NEW F — Fade back in: the young woman alone on the couch, looking at her phone]
MUG: ...Probably not.

[END CARD: Sterling — "Advice from a dying man"]
```

**Cut from v4:** shot 10 (the flashback), the walk-out narration, and the closing questions over the cold mug (15).
**New stills (6):** A, B, C, D, E and F. F no longer needs Sterling in the window: it's her alone, lit by the phone.
**Assembly:** Remotion, rendered in this repo.
