# Goodbye Coffee — Ep. 2 · Script v4 rev 3 (told by the mug)

The mug tells the whole story, as in Ep. 1. It narrates and quotes what Sterling and the young woman say; neither character has a voice of their own. One narrator voice (the Ep. 1 "Sterling" preset), plus the barista's single line.
New stills are marked **NEW**.

```
[01 — Door opens, snow]
The bell over the door rang, and the cold came in with him.
Sterling.
It's been a month since that day at the doctor's office.
A month doesn't sound like much.
It is when you're running out of them.

[02 — In line, on his cane]
Overcoat buttoned to the collar. Newsboy cap. A cane now.
He didn't have the cane last week, though he did have a limp.
His coat hung loose. His face had grown thin.

[03 — Barista pours]
The girl behind the counter filled me to the top, smiled, and said,
[04 — Mug slid across the counter]
BARISTA: "Careful. It's hot."

[05 — His hands around the mug]
He wrapped both hands around me.
His fingers were cold. And they shook.

[06 — Walk to the couch]
Tap of the cane. A pause. Another tap.

[07 — Sits, sets me down]
He lowered himself into the couch and opened his journal.

[NEW A — Young woman with her coffee at the other end of the couch]
The place was packed that morning.
A young woman asked if the seat next to him was taken.
He told her to go ahead.

[08 — Journal: "Devin"]
At the top of the page, he wrote one word.
Devin.
And he kept writing.

[NEW B — Close on her, glancing at the page]
She saw the name. Asked him who Devin was.

[NEW C — Two-shot, Sterling talking, still writing]
He didn't stop writing.
"My little brother," he said. "Haven't spoken to him in twelve years."

[14 — Journal page, pen pressing hard]
He was pressing so hard on the journal, I thought he was going to rip the paper.

[09 — Sterling at the snowy window]
He told her about their father's funeral.
Something said in the wrong tone, on the wrong day.
Something that could've been settled right there in the parking lot.
But he got in his car. And so did Devin.

[11 — Montage: chair, phone, invitation]
One missed Christmas became two.
Then a wedding he heard about from a cousin.
Twelve years.

[NEW D — Close on her, both hands around her cup, moved]
She asked him what the fight was about.

[12 — Close-up, wet eyes, at the window]

His eyes looked… [TO COME FROM YOU]
"I don't even remember," he said.

[13 — Cold mug]

[NEW E — Sterling closes the journal and turns to her]
```
