## Changes from the approved script, and why
- **03 and 04:** the smile goes on the pour, and "Careful. It's hot." goes on the slide across the counter, so each still gets its own beat.
- **06 and 07:** "made his way to the couch, sat down, and opened his journal" is split across the walk still and the sit-down still. The cane taps from v2 come back over the walk.
- **09:** the window still now has a line: "he didn't write anything for a long time."
- **10:** "in the parking lot, in the rain" matches the rain flashback.
- **11:** "Birthdays passed. Then a wedding." becomes "A wedding invitation sat unopened." The montage shows a chair, a phone and an invitation, but no birthday.
- **13:** "with so little time left…" moves onto the cold-mug shot, together with "His coffee went cold. He never noticed."
- **14:** the still already shows this handwriting on the page, so the narrator reads it aloud. It sets up the quote card, and the still doesn't need to be re-rendered.
- **15:** the still shows the journal left on the couch, so the mug says so. That also gives Ep. 3 a hook. If you'd rather he takes the journal with him, cut that line and re-render 15 without the journal.
- **No new stills needed.** Every line now lands on an existing image.
