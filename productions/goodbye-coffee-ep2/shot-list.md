# Goodbye Coffee — Ep. 2 "Resentment Collects Interest" (working title)

Format 9:16 · graphic-novel look (same as Ep. 1) · narrator: the mug, in first person · Sterling voice + piano score

## Continuity changes from Ep. 1
- **Season:** winter. Cold light, the window bright and white, frost.
- **Sterling:** a buttoned overcoat, a newsboy cap, and a **cane, which is new.** He's frailer than in Ep. 1: he moves slowly and pauses.
- **New character:** the barista, a young woman with dreadlocks tied up and a genuine smile.
- **Flashback look:** colder and grayer than the present. Rain, a cemetery parking lot, umbrellas. Same ink style.
- **Unchanged:** the blue mug with the pink G (the only saturated color), the leather journal (rust accent, now with a ribbon), the mustard couch, the brick and the bikes.
