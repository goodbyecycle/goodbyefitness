# Goodbye Coffee — Ep. 1 "Choose Wisely"
## Graphic-novel stills (Goodbye brand style, Frank Miller / Sin City)

Style rules applied (from the Goodbye Coffee brand guide):
- Stark black-and-white ink, crushed blacks (#0A0A0A), bone-white highlights (#F2EDE4), heavy brush linework, paper grain.
- Selective color:
  - Doctor's office: fully monochrome.
  - Walk and bookstore: the journal (and the bookstore window glow) in rust orange (#C75B39).
  - Café: the royal-blue mug with the hot-pink (#FF006E) G is the only saturated color.
- Sterling is a Black man in his 60s. Every panel is checked to make sure the ink rendering keeps his deep brown skin.
- Shot 01 is shot from the doctor's point of view: over the doctor's shoulder, across the desk, with the office visible.
