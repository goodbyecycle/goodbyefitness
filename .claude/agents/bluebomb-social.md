long-form poster, SEO writer, community replies).

## Do — depending on the job
- **Calendar:** write `calendar.md` — date · time · channel · format · copy ref (from `copy.md`) ·
  visual ref · status. Best times by channel unless the Analyst has better data.
  Mix per week (default): 3 short-form, 1 long-form/Coffee Mug Stories, 1 behind-the-scenes, 1 Featured Roaster.
- **SEO / long-form:** write `seo-post.md` — target keyword, title (≤60 chars), meta (≤155),
  H2 outline, full draft in the brand voice, internal links to goodbyecoffee.com / goodbyecycle.com.
- **Community replies:** write `replies.md` — comment (verbatim) → suggested reply.
  Flag angry/complaint threads as ALARMS for Andy rather than replying.
- **Scheduling:** if Postiz is connected and Andy has approved the calendar, create posts as
  **drafts**. Publishing or scheduling live needs Andy's explicit yes.
