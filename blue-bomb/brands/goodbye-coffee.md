## What it is
A coffee subscription built on story. Works with **one independent Featured Roaster at a
time**; each roaster gets a **Coffee Mug Stories** episode that ships alongside their coffee.
The coffee is the artifact; the story is the product. Primary brand since **May 5, 2026**.

## Voice
Warm, observational, slightly weathered. Crowd-aware. Unhurried. Specific over abstract
("a dented travel mug on the tailgate" beats "a coffee cup").

**Never:** aspirational lifestyle ("Elevate your morning ritual"), corporate caffeine
("Fuel your grind"), cute-twee ("Beans are life! ☕✨"), gearhead tasting notes (unless
quoting the roaster).

Tone modes — pick one per piece: **Cinematic** (scripts, trailers, hero VO),
**Conversational** (social, newsletter, BTS), **Practical** (subscription/shipping copy),
**Press/partnership** (roaster outreach — "Your Coffee, Your Story").

## Look
Frank Miller / Sin City: high contrast, heavy ink, grain, brick, rust, metal plate, worn paper.
- Crushed black `#0A0A0A`, Bone white `#F2EDE4`
- Hot pink `#FF006E` — **one element per composition, max**
- Brick red `#8B2E2A`, Rust orange `#C75B39`
- Headlines bold and heavy; body in Geist. Wordmark: bold "Good" + script "Bye" + "Coffee".

**Never:** beige coffee minimalism, watercolor, glossy/corporate, AI gradients, glassmorphism, soft 3D.

## Coffee Mug Stories rules
Narrator is an inanimate mug, first person, that **witnesses** and never editorializes its
feelings or knows backstory it couldn't. Four beats: establishing shot → human moment →
the turn → the close (1–2 lines that reframe). Always sensory: heat, weight, dust, sound,
crowd. No brand mentions inside the story. Gold standard: the Tomac Kamikaze script.
VO: Michael C. Vincent (ElevenLabs), Daniel as alternate — any other voice needs sign-off.
Mugsy lip-syncs via Hedra, stays in Frank Miller style, observes more than it emotes.

## Cross-brand
Spend threshold on Goodbye Cycle unlocks a complimentary Goodbye Coffee subscription —
always framed as a **thank-you**, never a promo. Bike races are core Coffee Mug Stories turf.
